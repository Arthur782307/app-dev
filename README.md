# My Favorite Series: The Walking Dead

## About the Series
**The Walking Dead** is an American post-apocalyptic horror television series about a group of survivors trying to stay alive in a world filled with zombies, also known as walkers.

## Why I Like It
I like this series because it has exciting scenes, unexpected twists, and interesting characters. It also shows the importance of survival, friendship, teamwork, and trust during difficult situations.

## My Favorite Characters
- Rick Grimes
- Daryl Dixon
- Michonne
- Glenn Rhee
- Negan

## My Favorite Quote
"We are the walking dead."
